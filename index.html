      Questions

      Frequently asked

      Which devices does Sunaar Pro run on?

      Android phones and tablets, Chromebooks, Android counter POS machines such as the PosiFlow PSF11, Windows PCs, and iPhone and iPad. It is coming soon on Google Play, Windows and iOS.

      Does it calculate making charges, wastage and GST?

      Yes. Enter the weight and purity and Sunaar Pro works out the gold value from today's rate, then adds making charges, wastage, stone value and GST on every line of the bill.

      Can I print bills?

      Yes. Print an A4 tax invoice, or an 80mm thermal receipt on a Bluetooth printer or the PSF11's built-in printer. You can also share the invoice PDF on WhatsApp.

      Can I use it on more than one device?

      Yes. Your shop data syncs between your devices, so the counter tablet and the owner's phone show the same stock and bills.

      Is my data safe?

      Your shop data is stored securely and only your shop's devices can see it. Read our privacy policy for details.

      Does it work on the PosiFlow PSF11?

      Yes. Sunaar Pro runs on the PSF11 all-in-one billing machine and prints receipts on its built-in 80mm thermal printer. Choose "Built-in" in the app's printer settings.

      Does it track HUID and hallmarking?

      Yes. You can save the HUID with each piece and it appears on your stock list and invoices.

      Is there a free plan?

      Yes. The Free plan costs nothing, forever: 1 device, up to 50 bills a month and 50 stock items, with billing, estimates, GST invoices and printing. Upgrade any time and all your bills and stock come with you.

      Do you have a plan only for girvi (money lending)?

      Yes. The Girvi plan is ₹299 a month, ₹2,990 a year or ₹7,990 for 3 years (+GST): complete Girvi Khata with customer photo and signature, item photos, simple or compound interest, receipts, packet labels, reminders and cloud backup, on 1 device. Need billing too? Pro includes Girvi Khata plus everything else.

      Can I pay monthly?

      Yes. Pay monthly, yearly (2 months free), or for 3 years at once (25% off, price locked). You can move to a longer period any time and your unused days carry over.

      What happens if I stop paying?

      Nothing is deleted. After 7 days of grace your shop moves to the Free plan: you can still open, search and reprint every old bill. Renew and everything opens again.

      Can you move my data from my old software?

      Yes, free. Export your stock, customers, girvi and old bills to Excel from your old software, and Sunaar Pro imports them.

      Is a refund possible?

      Try Sunaar Pro on the Free plan first. Paid plans are not refunded once started, but you can upgrade any time by paying the difference.
