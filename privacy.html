Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Sunaar Pro ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Sunaar Pro mobile app (package com.sjjewellers.sunaarpro) and the website sunaarpro.com (together, the "Service").

1. Who the app is for

Sunaar Pro is a business tool for jewellery shop owners and their staff. It is not meant for children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

2. Information we collect

Information you enter about your shop. Shop name, owner name, address, GSTIN, logo, mobile number and email you provide when setting up the app.

Business records you create. Stock items (weights, purity, HUID, prices, photos), bills and invoices, payments, customer details you record (such as name, mobile number and address, and PAN or Aadhaar where you capture it for high-value sales as required by law), supplier records, loans and pledges, and reports. You are responsible for having the right to record your customers' information.

Device information. A device identifier and basic device details so your shop's devices can sync and so we can limit how many devices are linked to your account.

Support messages. Anything you send us when you contact us for help.

3. App permissions

  • Camera: to scan barcodes and take product photos. Photos are only taken when you choose to.
  • Bluetooth and nearby devices: to connect to receipt printers, label printers and weighing scales.
  • Storage or files: to save and share invoice PDFs, reports and backups.
  • Internet: to sync your data between your devices and back it up.

You can turn off any permission in your phone's settings. Some features will stop working without it.

4. How we use information

  • To run the app: billing, stock, invoices, reports and syncing between your devices.
  • To keep your account secure and stop misuse.
  • To answer support requests and tell you about important changes to the Service.

We do not sell your data or your customers' data, and we do not use your business records for advertising.

5. Where your data is stored

Your data is saved on your device and synced to secure cloud servers run by our hosting provider (Supabase) so it is available on all your shop's devices. Data is sent over encrypted connections. Only devices linked to your shop can access your shop's records.

6. Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us run the Service (such as cloud hosting), when you choose to share something yourself (for example sending an invoice on WhatsApp), or when the law requires it.

7. How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete your shop's data from our servers within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep records longer.

8. Your choices and rights

You can view, edit and export your records in the app. To ask for a copy of your data or to delete your account and data, contact us using the details below.

9. Security

We use encryption in transit, access controls and regular backups to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so please keep your devices locked and your login details private.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app if the change is important.
