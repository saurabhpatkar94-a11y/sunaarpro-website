Questions

About plans and billing

How does the free trial work?

Basic comes with a 7-day free trial, and Pro, Business and Premium come with a 30-day free trial, all with every feature switched on. At the end of the trial, pick the plan that fits your shop and pay monthly or yearly.

What do I save by paying yearly?

Paying yearly costs the same as 10 months, so you get 2 months free. For example, Pro is ₹8,990 a year instead of ₹10,788 if paid monthly.

Is GST included in the price?

No. Prices exclude 18% GST, which is added to your invoice. Under each monthly price you can see the amount with GST.

What does "per shop" mean?

Each plan covers one shop and the number of devices shown on it. The Business and Premium plans let you run several shops in one account.
